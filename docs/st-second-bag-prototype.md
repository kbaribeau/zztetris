# Conditional Buri second-bag construction

This is an experimental source-guided extension, not a new reliability certification or a
change to the opening-to-maintenance transition.

## Source scope

[Buri's guide, section 2-1](https://note.com/buri_daicon/n/n03a28dbfe045)
explicitly puts the second-bag T in Left. Its full second-bag arrangement is an
example; the remaining pieces are improvised, not a universal seven-piece recipe.
The [pictured example](https://assets.st-note.com/img/1670505643694-kd4z6zKm0T.png)
has SHA-256 `42f4f0be833e959da2dc5fd91aa277a2b05238d64ef4a0b1581e3cceaf27bc64`.

The implementation includes exactly four conditional targets: that literal
SL-then-OJ Center arrangement and a **derived** OJ-then-SL arrangement, each on
an exact completed colored Buri01 or Buri02 base. The latter moves S/L upward
three rows and O/J downward three rows; its final occupied geometry is identical,
but its colored arrangement is not the literal picture. Data records provenance,
source-image and graph hashes, variant labels, base colors and target cells.

## Authority and boundaries

- A connected seven-lock certificate starts at an eligible observed empty first
  bag. Each accepted complete witness is matched to the actual pre-lock pose,
  actual event, and public post-lock state, including real preview replenishment.
- Merely importing a matching board, copying a disconnected history, or selecting
  a target ID does not authorize second-bag advice. Additional source locks form
  a connected replay chain, and retention requires live matching session state.
- Discovery sees only active, HOLD and at most six previews. Static edge witnesses
  are candidates, not authority: actual-pose replay, existing structural/notch
  guards, and full-prefix access checks remain mandatory and budgeted.
- A discovered terminal action whose successor is absent is **not** included in
  executable advice. Newly observed preview must be rechecked; universal spawn
  checks in offline graph generation are not a source of unseen pieces.
- Clear, incompatible/manual branches, reset/import, and completed target stop
  source construction as appropriate. Undo restores its saved public history, but its new revision must be revalidated;
  a disconnected second-stage certificate falls back rather than reviving advice.
  Completing either target leaves the opening active; only the pre-existing
  actual-clear handoff can enter maintenance. No allowances or quotas change.

The established first-seven source-pattern controls are separate. Any longer-play
comparison is development evidence only, not an extension of the existing formal
first-bag result. Independent review, browser checks and matched whole-play
controls passed their correctness checks, but later-play outcomes remain mixed.
Publication prioritizes valid source-guided openings, not a no-regression claim;
failed streams must not be used to tune thresholds into acceptance.
