# Finite Left-I scoring foundation

This is a separate, conservative **engineering contract**, not a general rule
that immediate TSDs beat I cleanup and not a new Center/Reset classifier.
It does not change the older Left-support / distinct replacement-I contract.

The ordinary `spent-cleanup-I-on-optional-work` guard remains unchanged.
A separate three-lock construction can certify a vertical I entirely on Left,
an actual Right Z cap, then a canonical full T-spin double using only the
original active/hold/preview. At least one originally missing Left cell in the
actual two cleared rows must be supplied by that I; a hypothetical slot or a
better Left surface does not qualify. Root Right demand must be only prospective
preparation, with no existing cleanup column. Other available-cap/cycle rules,
resource identity/history, actual movement, notches, sealed cells, regional
support and headroom remain checked. The original I cycle violation is retained
as evidence of the scoped construction rather than presented as ordinary policy.

Selection is late optional work after ordinary final admission. It can replace
only a freshly proposed I preparation without a concrete competing score or
Center goal. Existing retained commitments—including an outstanding prepared
Pocket or scoring schedule—are not replaced. The admitted incumbent is private
to the synchronous request and survives an unavailable/unknown/budget-limited
construction proof exactly. Search and repeated validation use the remaining
shared request nodes and original deadline, never a fresh allowance.

The finite origin, all three witnesses, connected resource history, and consumed
prefix are rederived during HOLD, movement, each lock and fresh advice. Later
reveals cannot be inserted into the original preview. Partial history stays
partial: this contract grants no Extra-Z, S/L, bag-age or Reset permission.
Imported kind/progress/telemetry fields are not authority.

The actual double can expose an aligned, cavity-free but unrecognized Center
motif. The finite construction explicitly reports that uncertainty; it does not
change the ordinary Center matcher or certify the next construction. Subsequent
advice must pass the existing current-position checks. Longer survival, a later
quad and another TSD are not implied by this three-lock proof.

The development regression preserves the recorded engine pre50 context and
literal I51/Z52/T53 route. The favorable local scoring fact is not an acceptance
result: full correctness, independent review and matched longer-run comparisons
are separate gates.
