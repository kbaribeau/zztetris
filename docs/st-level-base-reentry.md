# One-lock re-entry from a level-two Center base

The maintenance height gate could reject an already checked Center preparation
after the preceding double left a phase-aligned level-two base. The recorded
502400001 stop at lock 98 already carried a one-lock S preparation commitment;
it was not missing an S placement or asking for a new Reset strategy.

The new admission is deliberately smaller than ordinary maintenance search:

- The current validated scoring phase and exact `centerLevelBase` occupancy
  must establish a level-two Center. Left height is at least two, and Right is
  a cleared base. The generic maintenance/readiness predicates are unchanged.
- Complete actual-pose placement enumeration respects the rules profile and
  HOLD setting. A single nonclearing S placement must stay entirely in
  Center and return to strict regional support with a recognized reusable
  Center and aligned lower foundation.
- Existing notch, canonical-clear, cycle-resource and cavity certificates
  remain mandatory. No newly covered construction cell is admitted, excluding
  only validated protected notches. Final public access validation still runs
  and charges its work, including a failed or incomplete check.
- An actual reachable Z cap, including through HOLD, blocks this optional
  S preparation. This remains true on a cleared Right floor where the older
  geometric cap-demand classifier alone would not reserve that Z.
- A pending one-lock goal is reconstructed from its original public witness
  and matched to the same checked event/result. A different or longer goal,
  or another construction commitment, cannot be replaced by this shortcut.
- The result contains one lock, not a borrowed future score. Actual movement,
  HOLD, fresh worker requests and Undo preserve that goal; it retires after
  the real lock. Old suffix metadata cannot carry forward a completed double.

This is not a new phase, a generic low-stack exemption, a completed Center
pair, proof that a clear happened, or a Reset coach. The original covered Left
cell remains reported. The later uneven `[1,2,2]` base is still outside this
admission. The proposed S/L partner is geometric reference support, not a
promise about timely future supply.

## Evidence and validation

The [level-base diagnostic and reference scan](st-opening-repair.md#phase-aligned-level-base-diagnostic)
distinguish the phase-relative base from arbitrary flatness. The existing
Center grammar retains its source-backed motifs; no seed, recorded witness
or reference answer is used by production to select the move.

A frozen pre-change intervention on the exact lock-98 root forced S `R,HD`
and then resumed autonomous planning. It bought four placements and one
double before the next unsupported base. That is a local coverage lead, not
evidence of a completed repair or general reliability improvement.

`st-level-base-reentry.test.cjs` covers the actual incoming unmarked S goal,
fresh discovery, preserved T/I supply, unrelated/longer goals, malformed
history, partial bases, actual-pose/HOLD/legacy rules, exhausted budgets,
final-access accounting, final-candidate deadline crossing, and the real
page's worker/movement/lock/Undo request lifecycle. These are behavioral
script checks, not rendered-browser or indefinite-sustainability evidence.
