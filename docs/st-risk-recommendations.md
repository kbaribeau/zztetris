# Supported and risky midgame recommendations

This is an explicit recommendation class, not a relaxation of supported proofs.
A supported decision keeps the existing finite-prefix admission. Only when the
bounded supported search returns no target may `decide` reuse placements already
enumerated at that exact public root. Nested `plan` calls never return risk as a
certificate. No search budget is reserved, increased, or borrowed: fallback uses
remaining time/nodes and checks at most 64 existing candidates. All risk-path D
proof attempts share one4000node allowance within that same remaining global budget. Enumeration or
filter truncation remains disclosed uncertainty, not evidence no safer move exists.

A risky recommendation verifies one actual legal lock. Authentic resource history,
actual supply, notch integrity, canonical clears, ordinary Right roles and resource
permissions remain hard gates. The preceding branch A retained strict immediate usable J
for observed D even in its risk channel; branch B evaluates missing/unproved J as an
explicit risk instead. Risk may describe unresolved covered-cell restoration,
Center partner timing, unclassified allocation history or unsupported stack shape;
it never supplies a fabricated repair certificate or future piece.

Conservative prototype bounds: no newly sealed cavity, resulting height below19,
at most4 newly covered cells and6 tracked outstanding covered cells. These are
engineering bounds, not community rules or tuned thresholds. Rejected bounds are
reported in `metrics.riskFallback.rejections`; failure to qualify still abstains.
An eligible clean move may carry only continuation-proof uncertainty; requiring a
remaining structural hazard incorrectly excluded an exact known-L payment in the
first experiment. That original version and its evaluation are preserved. No
structural defect is invented to justify the risk label. Candidates are ordered by
outstanding covered debt, lost Center obligations, sealed cells, height, exact
authentic partner payments, actual lines, existing structural estimate, then input
length. Payment priority never overrides the preceding structural risk dimensions.
A payment matches the actual event to an authentic live root's exact target; it
does not grant a certified continuation or a spare-piece permission.
This is a local risk preference, not a global-best claim.

`recommendation.kind` is `supported` or `risky` on every returned `decide` target.
Risk includes `hazards` (`code`, `detail`, `downside`), search status/incompleteness
and a legal-placement-only claim. `riskBasis` is `uncertified-follow-up-only`
when that is the sole remaining uncertainty, otherwise `identified-dependency`.
These labels are diagnostics, never retained proof authority. No optional strict
one-lock upgrade is attempted here: existing revalidation would re-enumerate roots. Its status is `recommended-risky-placement` and
its text begins “Risky”. `certifiedHorizon` stays0; `legalHorizon` is1.

Risk is never retained as strategic authority: movement, lock, fresh Worker and
session import force supported-first recomputation. Session exports may preserve
it for human inspection, but imports discard it. No risky commitment or pending-L
certificate is created. Relabeling copied advice cannot satisfy existing supported
revalidation. No advisory Reset detector, Reset coach or execution handoff policy
is added here.


## Left contours and risk

D no longer creates a J-specific admission rule or warning. The recorded-contour
route search uses actual legal piece/contour stages with ordinary resource and
access checks. A failed or unfinished lookup does not itself create a hazard or
prove the position unplayable. The ordinary one-lock risk fallback still reports
actual unresolved Center, covered-cell, resource and continuation obligations.
Historical D fixtures remain useful for replay and those independent boundaries.
