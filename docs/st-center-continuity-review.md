## Bounded preference, not a new legality rule

A non-clearing placement that disrupts a reusable center creates a search-prefix
continuity flag. A later clear alone cannot erase it; an actually restored reusable
center can. Clear-only shape changes do not initiate it, and an imported unknown
center does not invent historical debt. This is separate from exact partner
reservations and is not persistent historical Reset state.

Completed candidate endpoints compare this flag after the existing safety,
regional-support and cycle-validity gates, before aggregate structural/tactical
ranking. Heuristic beam and qualification order stay unchanged. The continuity flag is
included in state deduplication so different repair histories do not merge. This
avoids forcing every intermediate state into a recognized shape or changing
unrelated ranking weights; a pruned repair can still be missed.

Reusable evidence requires both supported center grammar and a recognized local
status. A flat skyline alone does not prove the underlying motif restored. A
partial motif spanning more than three rows also cannot certify a deep missing
column just because its top three rows resemble a known pair. This is a bounded
local preference envelope, not proof of sustainable play or a full Reset model.
