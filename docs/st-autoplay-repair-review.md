# Autoplay structural repairs

The October 23 bounded autoplay review found two independently replayed errors.
These are development regressions now, not evidence of indefinite sustainability.

## Preserve a buried scoring cycle

“O filler before the next cap”, seed 10331, move 49 had a real scoring cap below
an O filler. The topmost silhouette was unrecognized, so a fresh worker request
forgot the column-seven phase and spent T across a protected notch. The same
visible state permits an immediate T-spin double, reducing maximum height from
18 to 16 instead of increasing it to 19.

The structure recognizer now checks the lower cap occupancy: its missing wall
cell beside the roof, solid wall below, scoring shaft and reservoir or prepared
cleanup I. Extra material above it does not erase that evidence. The analogous
buried bottom-I preparation retains its next-cap phase. This does not infer from
piece colors, absolute height modulo three, prior advice, a private generator,
or trusted imported phase metadata. Imports, movement, deviations, reset and
fresh worker observations all recompute from the actual board. Unknown geometry
still remains unknown; this is not a universal historical phase tracker. A checked
transition also rejects positively recognized geometry that contradicts the
translated established phase. The exact seeded validation exposed this when an
I in column nine preserved old blank teeth but established a different next-cap
phase; merely checking that no reserved cell was filled was insufficient.

Regression evidence includes the exact preceding filler and the new observation,
and the two other same-family phase-loss states already present in the original
audit (seeds 10333 and 10342). Recorded trajectories before their first actual
notch violation now retain the established phase on fresh observations.

## Do not issue a new sealed internal cavity

“Independent preparation”, seed 10333, move 14 put O on the left and sealed a
center cell at zero-based (4,30). A replayed O-center, I-column-two, I-column-one,
L-tuck alternative establishes that this damage was avoidable with visible
supply. The corrected engine need not choose that exact alternative: another
O-left placement in columns one–two avoids the seal as well.

New internal sealed-cell identities are tracked through actual line-clear row
translation. Clearing an unrelated existing cavity cannot cancel new damage.
Preexisting imported holes, side-accessible covered cells and the intentional
right-region reservoir are not blanket-rejected.

A concrete later repair may discharge debt inside a searched continuation, but
normal advice will not issue a first move carrying new unresolved internal debt.
That conservative boundary matters because a later worker request is not bound
to finish an old plan. No new history/import metadata was introduced to make
such a commitment persistent. Thus a legal but temporarily sealed construction
may remain uncertified even when a longer repair exists. Revalidation applies
the same boundary; failure means unsupported by this proof, not illegal or
mathematically irrecoverable.
