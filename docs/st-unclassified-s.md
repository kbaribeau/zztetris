# Unclassified S accommodation

Maintenance preserves replay-derived `hangingS.unclassified` separately from
confirmed surplus identities and exact pending Center targets. A matching closed,
invalidated SL root qualifies that record as `invalidated:true`. It binds caches,
current advice and revalidation; no obsolete target is restored.

The existing finite repair grammar cannot prove accommodation of an already
consumed S after its exact supported SL target was invalidated. It returns unknown
(`valid:null`, `unclassified-S-accommodation`), so ordinary certification abstains.
Opening construction itself remains unchanged. Undo to the pre-invalidation
position restores its pending target; current session imports recompute these facts.
No same-bag replacement or later unrelated S pays this historical uncertainty.

Generic unknown history creates neither confirmed debt nor an invented
invalidation. Other unclassified historical S allocations remain explicit in
resource evidence and bindings, but are not upgraded into an invalidated pair or
new blanket rejection. With no confirmed repair needed they report
`no-confirmed-resource-repair-needed`, preserving the distinction from settled
history. This avoids changing source-opening allocation policy based solely on
an unreviewed historical classification. A future explicit recovery model could
resolve invalidations; this change does not invent one or implement Reset.

The regression fixture contains public observations and replay context at seed
4242428105 before locks 17 and 18. I17 invalidates SL root14; history retains its
unclassified S, which previously disappeared in maintenance initialization.

Within a projected prefix, consuming a confirmed hanging S in an unreviewed
allocation records `requiresAccommodation:true` on its unclassified record. This
is neither a live piece nor a new confirmed placement target. It prevents loss
of an unmet obligation from turning a previously rejected prefix into a proven
repair. Completing another Center root cannot discharge it. Repair goals,
ordinary admission and revalidation all retain this guard, including uncertainty
created during the repair search itself. Actual lock replay also records whether the S was a confirmed surplus immediately
before disposal. History resolution carries that derived fact into the next
maintenance initialization and post-disposal session replay; Undo restores the
pre-disposal identity. Serialized summary flags are never trusted.

This does not reinterpret all already-unclassified imported/opening history as
confirmed debt. It is a guard against creating acceptable uncertainty from a
replay-proven known obligation, both within a projected prefix and across actual
locks. A historical unclassified allocation without that proven transition does
not acquire the flag.
