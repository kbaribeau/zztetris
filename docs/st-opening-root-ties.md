# Bounded opening root ambiguity

## Earlier cause, not a late-stack exemption

On development stream 502923646, before the third lock, two I-in-Left
placements had identical strategic rank components. Their only rank difference
was input effort. The reduced beam kept the shorter witness and lost the other
public board shape. Three locks of lookahead did not expose the useful I/O/T/J
continuation. These observations justify neither an I bonus nor a prescribed
four-piece sequence.

An isolated 2×2 diagnostic crossed original/tied-root coverage with depth three
or four at the same 45,000-node allowance. Extra coverage alone retained the
original three-lock choice. Depth four alone chose a different held-T root.
Together they exposed the useful I-left family. Fresh 1500 ms rooted
continuations gave 25, 25, 30 and 52 locks respectively. All 132 recorded locks
were independently replayed. The depth-four/ties run still stopped without a
Tetris; global depth four also changes existing closure eligibility and was not
promoted.

## Production admission

The ordinary depth-three policy, rank and original beam slots remain intact.
The optional family is limited to an established, phase-consistent, unfinished
open lower Center base with zero Left parity, no covered Left cell and no known
buried Center vacancy. Right must be an uncapped cleared floor, and Center must
already have geometric supported-partner-completion evidence. That does not mean
its current motif is recognized or that the partner is currently playable or
will arrive in time. Generic near-empty construction under a built cap is excluded. It is excluded when a repair is supplied, even an invalid
one. No seed, hidden future, fixed column or piece-type bonus enters admission.

At most two extra root placements may join the beam when an original retained
placement in the same first-piece/region/parity family has exactly the same
strategic rank vector, excluding only input effort. Both original and added
placements form the ambiguity group. This symmetry matters: following legal
inputs can change which witness is shorter without resolving the strategic
ambiguity. An added-only prototype changed targets after five inputs and was
rejected. The symmetric prototype preserved the exact target at all 25 input
poses under both fixed-node and 1500 ms checks, including separate HOLD checks.

One optional fourth layer is admitted only after a complete reduced-envelope
three-lock layer with a checked three-lock nominal result and a surviving member
of the ambiguity group. A saved repair, actual clear candidate or selected
visible closure takes priority. All retained competitors are compared normally
at equal depth; an unrelated final winner cannot justify this extension.

The extra layer is bounded by 6,000 nodes and 200 ms within the existing request
allowance. Those are extra-layer limits, not a claim about total added work:
the extra root coverage in nominal layers, subsequent handoff probes and final
public access validation also use the main request's remaining budget. An incomplete layer or access proof restores the
nominal advice and candidate-list lengths while retaining spent work and
reporting the incomplete extension. The final winner is checked after ordinary
handoff priority. Final public-access failure clears the selected flag too.

This is still reduced-envelope search, not exhaustive or optimal search.
Notch/canonical-clear/cavity/access checks are unchanged. A four-lock result
without an independent repair certificate creates no rigid multi-lock goal;
replanning may choose a better later route. Eligibility parity zero does not
require every candidate prefix to remain balanced.

## Verification and limits

Focused regressions cover the known rank tie, alignment/landing, actual page
HOLD and fresh worker requests, real lock and Undo, node/deadline rollback,
covered-Left and buried-Center exclusions, supplied repair exclusion and final
public-access selection accounting. Existing repair and visible-closure tests
also assert that they do not enter this family. A controlled selection-order
unit test substitutes a real searched competitor after handoff priority; this
checks rollback placement, not existence of an actual handoff in that fixture.

A preliminary adaptive prototype reached 68 locks, a handoff and 14 lines on the
thin-Center stream, with one Tetris but no joint goal. That is development-only
prototype evidence, not the final production comparison. Three maintenance
requests exceeded 1500 ms slightly; none was the opening extension. The original
750 ms restoration goal remains open. Final frozen production comparisons and
full-suite results must be reported separately.


## Rejected generic candidate

The broader `7e7d9da` candidate passed 576 behavioral tests and focused target-pose
checks, but its frozen five-stream comparison regressed overall: 426 to 303
locks and three to one joint milestones. The useful 502923646 run improved from
25 to 68 locks, while 502400001 fell from 102 to 13 after a selected fourth-layer
S-Center choice at lock three. That root was an almost empty, unrecognized Center
under an already built cap, not the uncapped partner-supported family above.
The failed result is retained, not replaced by a retry.

503237833 separately fell from 106 to 29 without ever attempting root ties or
this extension. Its first move differed alongside different coverage in the pre-existing timed
foundation-stage search. This is consistent with sub-budget cutoff sensitivity,
but causality was not isolated. Narrowing this new feature cannot be credited
with resolving that separate failure.
The other two streams were identical. A narrower structural trial requires a
new frozen comparison and cannot turn these failures into a reliability claim.

## Final narrowed development comparison

The fresh, predeclared five-pair comparison of `8c5bbb9` and `8c61234` finished
before the final-review boundary. All runs used 1500 ms, 45,000 nodes, a 2500 ms
VM watchdog and a 150-lock cap, alternating serial arms from empty. No run was
retried or replaced. All 895 recorded locks, public requests, actions and source
hashes were independently verified.

- 502923646: 25 to 68 locks, zero to 14 lines. The first difference is I at lock
  three. Maintenance starts after 16 locks (first maintenance-advised placement
  is 17), with one Tetris but no joint milestone.
- 503552020, 502609459, 502400001 and 503237833: every action, event and public
  state is unchanged at 68, 125, 102 and 106 locks respectively. The saved
  level-base S at 99 remains intact.
- Totals: 426 to 469 locks; four to five handoffs; joint milestones stay three
  of five. Every run eventually abstains, with no invalid recommendation,
  watchdog failure or censoring. Only the target's I3 request attempts and
  selects the extra layer; no regression stream attempts it.

Three later target maintenance requests still take about 1509–1548 ms, beyond
the soft allowance but below the watchdog. They do not use the opening
extension. The original 750 ms goal is not restored. These selected development
streams are not a >99% reliability estimate or held-out acceptance campaign.
The lower Center remains phase-aligned through the target's 68 locks; later
Left parity/coverage and nonclearing accumulation remain diagnostic work, not
proof that one observed move caused the stop.

The exact `8c61234` runtime passed all 25 pre-HD target poses and both HOLD
states under 1500 ms, with complete witness/event replay (maximum 405 ms and
7,235 nodes). Its clean full suite passed 578/578 cases in 118.399 seconds using
the cold per-run compilation cache; the 15-second file and 120-second parent
watchdogs remain unchanged. The earlier uncached 120-second cancellation is
preserved as a failed aggregate run, not silently counted as a pass.
