# Column-seven future-notch protection review

Scope: the approved ordinary ST two-filled / one-empty cadence, anchored by
recognizable scoring, cap-preparation, or cleanup geometry. This is a hard
recommendation constraint, not a scoring bonus or a retuned height tie-break.
The rules kernel still permits manual placements. No playable fixtures were added.

## Structural evidence

- A scoring wall requires a real two-cell outer roof, not just matching heights.
- A solid outer cleanup I is cap-pending even when the left scoring rows are
  unfinished. It must not be mistaken for a Z roof.
- A real scoring roof takes precedence when cleanup is also available.
- Uncapped cleanup foundations retain the next-Z phase. Empty right-floor rebuilds
  obtain a phase from an actual hypothetical Z-drop witness.
- The current arm can be occupied by its clearing T-spin double. Other reserved
  column-seven cells must stay empty. Missing upper construction cells are allowed.
- Reserved rows are translated through actual clear rows, carried in search-node
  identity, and checked throughout preparation proofs, advance, and revalidation.
- Fresh roots reconstruct the same phase as the inherited checked sequence; this
  prevents losing the constraint between locks or after a quad.
- Unknown geometry remains unanchored. Already-broken recognized patterns abstain;
  there is no newly implemented recovery mode or proof of indefinite survival.
