# Handoff-aware opening repairs

This changes only the opening phase. Maintenance notch policy and broad opening weights are not retuned.

## Candidate clears

A canonical clear is still a legal candidate, not automatically a useful opening outcome. Its existing tactical line credit is gated by balanced Left cells, a supported Center, established notches and an aligned lower Center foundation. Up to two candidate post-clear states receive a maintenance search using only their remaining public supply. A successful probe can prioritize the finite opening prefix; it never changes the session phase. Actual execution and a fresh handoff check remain mandatory.

Search and probes share the same absolute deadline and node ceiling. When a clear is plausible, up to 120 ms and 6,000 nodes are reserved for probes. An unprobed or bounded-search miss is unknown, not proof of impossibility. Metrics distinguish probe status and the opening search limit.

## Repair commitments

Only replay-verified finite improvements are retained: cancellation of observed Left checkerboard imbalance into a supported Center, or a concrete recognized Center partner completion. An unseen future T or a scalar foundation score cannot authorize a commitment. The original public observation and at most four actual placement witnesses define the proof.

After each real lock, the proof's event, board, active/hold and known queue prefix must match. The goal is retired when actually completed. Exact Center cells translate through canonical clears; an unrelated clear or different cavity cannot masquerade as the specified partner completion. Following a saved HOLD/movement/rotation prefix is supported, including rotation evidence. A nonmatching manual path may conservatively discard the proof. Imports and resets cannot inject history; Undo restores the saved session for fresh replay validation.

A fresh replacement must fulfill the same pending goal and improve the existing rank at an equal completed-lock horizon, or provide a checked complete handoff satisfying that goal. Replacement certificates retain validated goal authority, not an earlier unrelated improvement. Authority lineage is capped at four; otherwise the existing safe proof is retained. This is deliberately incomplete, and beam-pruned alternatives are not declared worse.

### Per-request raw board reuse

Opening search reuses raw board features and assessments for immutable board arrays within one synchronous request. This avoids recomputing the same parent flood/slot analysis for every child. It does not share the maintenance planner's proof-decorated assessments or cache inherited phase authority by occupancy. Final certificate replay remains independent, and the caches disappear at the end of the request.

A six-position fixed-node comparison retained exactly the same witnesses, continuations, structural evidence, ranks' selected outcomes, node counts and completed depths. Timing differences are a performance observation, not a new strategy or survival claim; production wall-limited selection may still change when more search completes.

### Checked one-lock foundation stages

The opening beam can retain one additional checked construction child for its next lock layer. This addresses an observed coverage loss: O-center then I-left leaves a temporary two-cell lower-Center gap; the actually visible J can complete it, and the three-lock endpoint already wins the existing rank, but the unfinished two-lock parent was outside the ordinary beam. No rank weight or piece-order bonus is added.

The bounded stage uses an established, independently regenerable notch phase and one to four exact missing Center foundation cells below the existing top level. It tries a small actual active/HOLD straight-drop witness family. The completion must fill those cells, produce a level aligned lower foundation without raising the existing maximum height, preserve Left parity and Right occupancy, and pass ordinary notch, seal and full-prefix movement-access checks. A geometric fit alone is not enough. Search misses are unknown, not impossibility results.

Ordinary beam slots remain intact. The checked child competes only at the next completed-lock horizon, not against shallower nodes using its future score. Stage discovery shares the request's time/node budget and is capped at2,000 work units, six eligible parents per layer and40ms per production layer; fixed-node diagnostics without a wall deadline remain node-bounded. Directed witnesses are incomplete and do not represent exhaustive legal movement search.

A selected stage carries a distinct finite-foundation goal through actual locks, HOLD, preview refresh and Undo until its exact completion is executed. Reset/import/manual deviation cannot inject or blindly preserve its authority. This does not change the global reusable-Center predicate: the small completed foundation may still have an unrecognized top motif and is not a completed opening, maintenance handoff or Reset.

### Phase-aligned level-base diagnostic

A separate diagnostic recognizes a solid Center base ending immediately below the current validated notch anchor: every Center cell below the anchor is occupied and every Center cell at or above it is empty. This is called a `phase-aligned-level-base`, not a claim that a historical clear occurred. Equivalent imported occupancy can be classified from freshly recognized geometry; a supplied phase is only meaningful after the caller has validated its history.

A bounded static scan of the recorded Sprint, Ultra, mirrored Marathon and1000-line references found46 aligned but motif-unrecognized post-T-double Centers; all46 have this exact phase-relative shape and restore recognized support within one to five recorded placements. Original input/hold/preview are not reconstructed. Frozen controls include Ultra256 and1000-line553, wrong phase, arbitrary flatness, buried debt, color invariance and the legal but uneven I7 post-clear surface. The latter is open construction, not a level base and not automatically damage.

This diagnostic does not change ranking, score credit, full motif recognition, Left parity, Right cap/cleanup duties, repair-goal completion or maintenance handoff. A recognized Center base can coexist with an unsafe Left region. Its purpose is to distinguish phase-aligned rebuilding from a genuinely damaged Center before any separately validated strategy change.
