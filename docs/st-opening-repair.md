# Handoff-aware opening repairs

This changes only the opening phase. Maintenance notch policy and broad opening weights are not retuned.

## Candidate clears

A canonical clear is still a legal candidate, not automatically a useful opening outcome. Its existing tactical line credit is gated by balanced Left cells, a supported Center, established notches and an aligned lower Center foundation. Up to two candidate post-clear states receive a maintenance search using only their remaining public supply. A successful probe can prioritize the finite opening prefix; it never changes the session phase. Actual execution and a fresh handoff check remain mandatory.

Search and probes share the same absolute deadline and node ceiling. When a clear is plausible, up to 120 ms and 6,000 nodes are reserved for probes. An unprobed or bounded-search miss is unknown, not proof of impossibility. Metrics distinguish probe status and the opening search limit.

## Repair commitments

Only replay-verified finite improvements are retained: cancellation of observed Left checkerboard imbalance into a supported Center, or a concrete recognized Center partner completion. An unseen future T or a scalar foundation score cannot authorize a commitment. The original public observation and at most four actual placement witnesses define the proof.

After each real lock, the proof's event, board, active/hold and known queue prefix must match. The goal is retired when actually completed. Exact Center cells translate through canonical clears; an unrelated clear or different cavity cannot masquerade as the specified partner completion. Following a saved HOLD/movement/rotation prefix is supported, including rotation evidence. A nonmatching manual path may conservatively discard the proof. Imports and resets cannot inject history; Undo restores the saved session for fresh replay validation.

A fresh replacement must fulfill the same pending goal and improve the existing rank at an equal completed-lock horizon, or provide a checked complete handoff satisfying that goal. Replacement certificates retain validated goal authority, not an earlier unrelated improvement. Authority lineage is capped at four; otherwise the existing safe proof is retained. This is deliberately incomplete, and beam-pruned alternatives are not declared worse.

## Frozen controls and interpretation

Round 1 used unchanged b0b1767, 50 predeclared streams and 70-lock caps. Its 39 handoffs met the old mechanical gate. Five have non-notch Center vacancies below the shared surface, so only 34 of those frozen endpoints satisfy the tightened foundation criterion. This is retrospective reclassification, not a new measured 34/50 success rate or a claim that the five boards are impossible. All original actual clear/continuation witnesses remain legal. Four reference first-double foundations pass the new check; unfinished supported partner shapes at the top are still allowed.

The exact lost repair after seed 502818917 lock 11 is a public-information regression: the previously checked cancelling T remains legal and restores parity zero. Another safe recognized Center order may replace the recorded S follow-up; the policy does not encode the seed or replay page. Tests include all 39 original handoff proofs, eight failed-clear gates, 11 preserved failure snapshots, manual/Undo lifecycle and a synthetic-supply reference-board control against premature retirement at an unrelated Center completion.

The implementation does not claim full Reset control, sustained maintenance, or greater-than-99% reliability. The agreed eventual opening acceptance outcome also requires two Tetrises with intervening T-spins after handoff. A large held-out acceptance run is separate from these development controls.

## Bounded development comparison

Candidate `27d4cb7` passed the clean 475-test suite; independent focused review passed 36 tests. Four predeclared existing Round 1 inputs were replayed with identical streams/settings and 70-lock caps, no retries. All 193 executed candidate locks and three actual-clear handoffs were independently replay-verified. Results are preserved in [the comparison data](st-opening-repair-results.json).

- 502818917: previously no handoff and abstention after 29 locks; now handoff at 24 and abstention after 33. No post-handoff Tetris.
- 503237833: previously no handoff and abstention after 31; now handoff at 19 and reaches the 70-lock cap, with six subsequent T-spin doubles and one Tetris. The cap is censored, not proof of unlimited survival.
- 506379703: previously the shallow-foundation handoff at 22 followed by a policy stop after 49; now no handoff and opening abstention after 37. Avoiding the late policy failure is not a survival improvement or a demonstrated recovery.
- 502504730: both hand off at 15, but the candidate abstains after 53 versus 59, with three post-handoff T-spin doubles and no Tetris versus the baseline's three doubles and one Tetris. This is a regression.

There were no returned invalid recommendations, actual top-outs, policy stops or runner errors in these four candidate runs. None meets the new joint two-Tetris outcome. These mixed development results support the exact repair and stricter handoff semantics, not a general performance or reliability claim. Wall-clock search remains incomplete and timing-sensitive: maximum observed planning including runner overhead was approximately 825 ms despite the cooperative 750 ms planning budget. Historical baseline timing is not a controlled simultaneous benchmark. No further strategy tuning was performed to hide these outcomes.
