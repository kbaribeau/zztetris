# Phase-aligned Center construction

The Center's top three rows are evidence of a local combination, not evidence that its lower foundation is complete. `STStructure.centerFoundation` now checks the entire region below the common Center surface against the positively established scoring-notch phase. Actual protected column-seven notches are allowed. Other missing cells remain an unfinished foundation. An unanchored phase is explicitly unknown, never guessed from colors or a convenient modulo.

This strengthens the existing completed-endpoint continuity preference. It is **not** a hard ban on temporary departures, a new legality rule, or a universal ST classifier. In particular, reference J/Z construction and longer Marathon intermediate departures remain executable. Static full-reference review found the same preference incidence for Sprint, Ultra and the 1000-line reference; the final Marathon departure begins one lock earlier under the stronger criterion. The finite ending does not establish that the recorded move is universally wrong.

## Community grounding and limits

[FOUR's ST building explanation](https://four.lol/stacking/st/#building) describes reusable center combinations and notch spacing, while allowing flexible boundaries. [Flamewheel's Center Stacking guidance](https://tl.net/forum/games/175252-10000-guide-tetris-ultra-st-stacking) describes paired combinations with the lower-right opening and a level three-wide top. These support checking construction rather than rewarding a merely plausible skyline; neither source certifies this exact implementation or its long-run performance.

No height, top-out, cavity or notch safety guard is relaxed. The seven-lock cleanup counterexample that temporarily reaches height20 remains uncertified by the coach. There is no new Reset policy, opening script, private-stream access or forced identical recommendation order. Diagnostic simulation remains necessary, and development seeds do not establish the agreed held-out reliability goal.
